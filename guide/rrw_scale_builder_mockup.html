A scale is chosen from the Type menu, just after its number type, and saves as that type plus the scale mode: Integer (scale) is an Integer, Decimal (scale) a Decimal. Whether it snaps or slides depends only on how many points it has, so a decimal scale snaps too (0 to 1, Step 0.1: 11 points). It needs a Min, a Max and a Step. A scale row takes a second line, under its name, type and bounds, for its three optional labels and, after them, what the reviewer will get: up to 11 points a scale that snaps, more a slide with its box. The three labels are optional; the numbers always show.
