A scale answer for numeric fields — mockup

An Integer or Decimal response field with both a Min and a Max, set by the operator to answer as a scale, shows as a line with a circle: up to 11 points it snaps to each one; past 11 it slides. Click a point, drag the circle, or use the arrow keys. Nothing is answered until the reviewer chooses.

On the reviewer surface (desktop, Responses across)

Two scale fields beside a text answer. Row 1 is unanswered; row 2 is answered. The value the form submits is shown under the table.

More than 11 points: a slide, no snaps shown

Past 11 points the stops would sit too close to tap, so the line shows none. The circle follows the finger smoothly; the value still lands on the field's Step, and a readout above the circle says what it is while dragging (and while focused). Only Min, the midpoint and Max are numbered. Arrow keys move one step; Page Up / Page Down ten.

A. Slide with its readout. Integer 0–100, Step 1 (101 points).

Decimal 0–10, Step 0.1 (101 points), answered.

B. Slide with a box beside it, for an exact value: typing moves the circle, sliding fills the box.

Integer 1,000–2,000, Step 5 (201 points), with a box.

On a phone a 300 px line holding 101 points gives each point about 3 px, so a finger lands near a value, not on it: the readout shows where it landed, and the arrow keys or the box (B) make it exact.
