Reviewees are the people being reviewed. They may be the same people as the reviewers, as in a peer review. A session must have at least one reviewee to proceed. Validation will block you.

A reviewee need not be identified by an email address at all, if you are only collecting data about them and not showing them the collected data. If you need them to see the responses or response summaries, they must be identified by an email address tied to their institutional MS365 account, as they will need it to log into the app.

The three tag columns are what assignment rules filter on, so put the attributes that decide who is being reviewed by whom there (e.g., tutor, tutorial group).

Uploading a CSV replaces the whole roster and clears any assignments already generated. To take someone out of a running session without losing their rows, mark them inactive instead of deleting. You can also add individual reviewees on this page.

See Create and set up a session in the Guide.
