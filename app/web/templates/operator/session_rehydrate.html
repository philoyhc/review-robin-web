Rehydrate an extracted session

Rebuild a past session — its settings, people, and all collected responses — from a complete set of extract files.

You need reviewers.csv, reviewees.csv, settings.csv, and the CSVs from Extract data → “Extract all data” (the responses bundle). If your session used relationships or observers, include relationships.csv / observers.csv too. You can also just upload the two ZIP bundles.

Restored: settings, reviewers, reviewees, observers, relationships, assignments (regenerated), and submitted responses. Not restored: invitations, email send history, and participant results-acknowledgements.

The rebuilt session is named <original name>_REHYD and lands as a draft — you review and activate it yourself.
