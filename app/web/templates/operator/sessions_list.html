A session is one review round with its own people, form, dates, and results.

{# Tiles are `.card.rs-help-card` — the app's existing help-card semantics — rather than a tile look of their own. A row of tiles inside a card is explaining something, which is what that style already says. `.stepped` puts a `→` between them: these are four stages in order, not four parallel options. The arrows are `aria-hidden` and carry no text — the sequence is already in reading order, and "right arrow" announced three times between four headings is noise. They hide below 900px, where the row wraps and a horizontal arrow would point at nothing. #}

Set up a session

Create one using the Add new session button above. Add reviewers, reviewees, and choose the questions they answer.

→

Prepare and activate

Generate the assignment pairs, run the pre-flight check, fix anything blocking, then activate the session to receive responses.

→

Give reviewers access

Share the app's address; people sign in and see the reviews that apply to them.

→

Download responses

At the close of the session, download the collected data.
