Reviewers are the people who fill in the form. They may be the same people as the reviewees, as in a peer review. A session must have at least one reviewer to proceed. Validation will block you.

The email address is mandatory for the app to identify the person. It should also be tied to their institutional MS365 account, as they will need it to log into the app.

The three tag columns are what assignment rules filter on, so put the attributes that decide who reviews whom there (e.g., tutor, tutorial group).

Uploading a CSV replaces the whole roster and clears any assignments already generated. To take someone out of a running session without losing their rows, mark them inactive instead of deleting. You can also add individual reviewers on this page.

See Create and set up a session in the Guide.
