{# Page-level guidance disclosure — Segment 19E rung 6. One per Setup page, above the page's cards. Closed by default: an operator who already knows the page should not scroll past a paragraph they have read, and one who does not gets it in one click. Native
, no JS — the same primitive the Validate page's per-issue "Why this check?" already ships (``partials/validation_results.html`` -> ``.issue-why``), one level up. The plan rejected tooltips (cannot hold a sentence, invisible to touch, awkward for keyboard) and the ``.banner`` family (specced behaviourally as transient page-level feedback, which persistent explanation is not). The summary text is fixed rather than a parameter: this is a repeated affordance across six Setup pages, and an operator learns a control faster when it is worded identically everywhere. A page that genuinely needs different wording is a finding about the scaffold, not something to vary quietly. Body content comes through ``{% call %}`` so each page writes its own prose while the chrome stays in one place. Keep that prose short and **link to /guide rather than restating it** — the Guide is the canonical documentation, and two copies of the same explanation is the drift this segment exists to stop.
