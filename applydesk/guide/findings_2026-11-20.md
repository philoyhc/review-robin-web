## From the codebase assessment

The assessment's package read (`guide/codebase_assessment_20nov.md` in RRW): a
route fuzz of every GET route under six identities, a mutation fuzz of the
saves, and the suite (681 passed). None of these is reachable in production by
anyone but the platform super-admin, and Apply Desk is not deployed.

- ~~**S8**~~ — **Fixed in #PENDING**: a gate number's digits are bounded (a 404), and an offset is read from its first 12 characters (it clamps to the last page). (low, code, CONFIRMED) — a path number or `offset` of more than 4,300
  digits answers 500 (`ValueError`, Python's integer-string limit), not 404 or
  page one: `routes_operator.py:480` (`_gate_or_404`, which serves `{kind}.csv`
  too), `:609`, `:735`. All 66 of the fuzz's 500s were this.
- ~~**S9**~~ — **Fixed in #PENDING**: a non-string kind, type or key is refused (400). (low, code, CONFIRMED) — a list or object where a JSON string is
  expected raises `TypeError: unhashable` and answers 500: a Form card's `type`
  (`routes_operator.py:257`), a Panels panel's `key` (`scratch_panels.py:121-125`).
  `spec/applications.md:174-181` says 400.
- **S10** (low, author) — the scratch store has no global cap: each cookieless
  POST to `/operator/exercises/new` mints a browser entry (300 posts, 301
  entries); bodies are read whole before the `MAX_BODY` check
  (`routes_operator.py:82`, `:182`, `:411`); `_prune` walks every browser under
  the lock on each request. 40 browsers × 5 exercises took RSS from 114 to 193
  MiB. Matters only if Apply Desk ships before AD2 replaces the store. Isolation
  and thread-safety are sound.
- **S11** (low, author) — `/operator/exercises?said=<text>` shows any text in the
  status line, escaped: content spoofing, not XSS (`routes_operator.py:57`,
  `exercises.html:11`). Keep the flash pattern, or carry a message key.
- **S12** (low, author) — the gate's four bundle CSVs (`handoff.py:90-95`) have
  no formula guard, though they are downloads on the gate page
  (`routes_operator.py:564-577`) and `extract.py:171` guards its own. They are
  Review Robin's import contract, so a guard changes the bytes it reads: guard
  the download only, or accept. Smaller: the Dossiers and Panels client-side CSV
  builders quote on `[",\n]` but not `\r` (`_dossiers.html:854`,
  `_panels.html:658`).
