UTF-8, one header row, up to 500 rows and 1 MiB. The addresses are read from the email column; if there isn't one, you choose the column next. Every other column comes back unchanged.

What it does and doesn't do

  • It reads the directory only. Nothing in the directory, Review Robin or Residential Apps is changed; you make any corrections yourself.
  • Nothing you upload is kept: the result is a download, and the file is gone once you leave the page.
  • An address the directory doesn't find is not proof that it's wrong. A shared mailbox, a mailing list or an alias may not be found.
  • It doesn't tell you whether someone can sign in, or which address an application will see when they do.
