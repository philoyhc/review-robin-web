# Support Logs: who can see what

*For the staff who use Support Logs. Agreed 2026-11-07. Parts of this are
still being built: the app shows them as placeholders until they work.*

Support Logs keeps short records of the support you give residents: who
you saw, when, roughly what about, and any follow-up. Those records are
about students at a vulnerable moment, so the app is built around one
idea: **you see what you need to do your job, and nothing more by
default.** Seeing more always takes a deliberate step, and the app
records that step.

## Your own records

**Every record you log is yours.** Other RFs and RSLMs cannot see it.
Senior staff can, as described below, and so can anyone you choose to
share it with.

## Seeing someone else's records

There are four ways, and each one is deliberate. Nobody gets access to
another person's records by accident or by default.

### 1. Sharing past records

You can share some or all of your past records about a student with one
colleague, for example someone covering for you.

- **They must accept the share before they see anything.** Until then
  they see only that you have shared records about a student, not who
  the student is.
- **You can withdraw a share** at any time.
- **A share covers only records that already exist.** Anything you log
  afterwards stays yours. To work together going forward, form a team.

### 2. Sharing as you log

When you log an encounter, you can share that one record with a
colleague as you save it. It's an optional detail, folded away so it
never slows down a quick log. Your colleague accepts it the same way.

### 3. Working as a team

When two or more of you share responsibility for a student, you can form
a team for that student.

- **Each of you accepts when the team forms.** The team exists once at
  least two people have accepted; anyone who declines stays out.
- **You decide whether the team sees your earlier records** about the
  student. That choice is yours alone, made as you join.
- **Records made while you are in the team belong to the team**: every
  member at that moment can see them, then and later.
- **The team can share a plan or follow-up**, so you keep up one
  follow-up between you.
- **The team's students appear on your Students page**, under **Teams**.
- **You can leave a team at any time.** A team down to one person ends.
  After that, you see your own records and the team records made while
  you were in it, and nothing else.

Senior staff can start a team, for example when a student needs several
people at once. Each member still accepts. A senior is not part of the
team unless they join it themselves.

### 4. Handing over

When a student moves from one staff member to another for good, for
example when an RF changes houses or leaves, the records can be handed
over.

- **The handover can be started** by the staff member whose records they
  are, or by a senior, choosing which records to hand over.
- **The chosen records become the new staff member's.** The previous
  staff member no longer has them.
- **Three people accept it:** the new staff member, the previous one and
  a senior. Whoever started it has already accepted.
- **If someone cannot be reached**, for example because they have left,
  a system administrator can accept on their behalf. The app records
  that it was done this way. Doing so does not let the administrator
  read any records.

## What senior staff see

Senior staff, such as the Master, oversee support across the team:
workload, who may need more help, and where support should move.

- **They can see every record when they need to**, for example to make
  sure a student is not falling through the cracks.
- **By default they see a student's name only when the student needs
  attention:** the student is on a formal plan, has a safety flag, or has
  a team.
- **Other students are not named until a senior chooses to look.** The
  overview shows "No formal plan" with **Show name**. Choosing to show a
  name is recorded.

## What other staff see

Office staff and others see students only if they have been given a
specific permission for their role. For example, someone coordinating
consultation checks sees what that task needs. Some permissions show
only counts and never a name.

System administrators set up the app and who may use it. They do not see
students or records.

## Safety flags

A safety flag goes to the people who need to act on it, whatever else is
or isn't shared. Sharing, teams and handovers never change who sees a
flag.

## Everything is recorded

The app keeps an audit log of who did what:

- every share, acceptance, decline and withdrawal;
- every team formed, joined, left or ended;
- every handover and each acceptance of it;
- every time a senior shows a student's name or opens a student's
  history;
- every download, upload and purge of records.

Senior staff review this log. The point is not suspicion: the log means
anyone can show that a student's information was handled properly.

## Quick answers

**Can my colleague in the next tower see what I logged?**
No, unless you share it with them, or you are both on a team for that
student.

**I'm going on leave. How does my cover see my notes?**
Share your past records about the students they will see. If you will be
working together going forward, form a team instead.

**I shared records by mistake.** Withdraw the share. If your colleague
has not accepted yet, they never saw the student's name.

**Can the Master read everything I write?**
In principle, yes: oversight is part of the role. In practice, students
who don't need attention are not named unless the Master chooses to
look, and that choice is recorded.

**What happens to my records when I leave?**
They are handed over to whoever takes on your students, with your
acceptance, or an administrator's if you cannot be reached.
