{# 4. Appeals beyond entitlement (spec §7). #}

Appeals beyond entitlement

When appeals are on, an RA with no entitlement left, or applying into an Appeal-only blackout, may ask the Master for extra leave. The Master decides personally; extra leave granted is kept apart from the entitlement (spec §7.5).

{{ choice("appeals", "Appeals beyond entitlement are", ("On", "Off"), "On" if leave.POLICY.appeals else "Off", {"On": "Offered to an RA with no entitlement left or applying into an Appeal-only blackout, on the terms below", "Off": "Not offered anywhere: an application beyond entitlement cannot be submitted, and Appeal-only blackouts refuse leave"}) }}

Extra duties are taken on after the leave is granted. They cannot be done in advance and banked against a future appeal.

{# 6. Approvals. #}

Approvals

An application takes a first approval, unless none is needed, then a final one, by different people. The slot's approvers are read from the RA's slot on Assignments for the current term; a delegate who approves first while an approver cannot is set on Approval chain.
