{# 4. Appeals beyond entitlement (spec §7). #}

Appeals beyond entitlement

When an RA has no entitlement left, or applies into an Appeal-only blackout, they may ask the Master for extra leave. The Master decides personally; extra leave granted is kept apart from the entitlement (spec §7.5).

Extra duties are taken on after the leave is granted. They cannot be done in advance and banked against a future appeal.
