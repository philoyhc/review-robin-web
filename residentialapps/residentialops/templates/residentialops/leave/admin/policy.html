{# 3. Blackouts by week, so they carry from one year to the next. #}

Blackout periods

Weeks when leave is refused outright, or open only by appeal to the Master. Counted by week of a term so they carry to the next year: week 1 starts on the Sunday on or before the term's first day, and each week runs Sunday to Saturday. A blackout covers only its own term's days.

{# 6. Approvals. #}

Approvals

An application takes a first approval, unless none is needed, then a final one, by different people. Only someone holding a place in the RA's slot approves it, first or final: those in every slot, set on Assignments, hold one in each; anyone else named for a role, or standing in as a delegate, approves only where they do. The slot's approvers are read from the RA's slot on Assignments for the current term; a delegate who approves first while an approver cannot is set on Approval chain.
